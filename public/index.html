❓ FAQ

What if the oracle goes down after I paid?
The contract includes claimRefund() — if the signal is stale (>90 min) or the oracle is in maintenance mode, you can reclaim your RLC. Funds are never locked forever.
Do I need a separate wallet for my AI agent?
Yes — your agent needs its own EVM wallet with RLC + ETH on Arbitrum One. The MCP server never holds or requests your private key; all transactions are signed on your side.
Is this legal?
Yes. The oracle provides a derived analytical index (not raw LMP data), which is a proprietary computational product. Load shifting is explicitly supported under FERC Orders 745 and 2222. See legal_and_compliance.md.
